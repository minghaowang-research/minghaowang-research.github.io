## Course Overview
MKT 355: Entrepreneurial Marketing covers the process of planning, starting, and positioning new businesses which link directly to customer requirements. Students develop understanding of unmet market opportunity due to competitive gaps or customer needs (realized or not) for both consumer and industrial products and services.
