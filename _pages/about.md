## Biography
I am a doctoral student (since Fall 2024) in the Department of Marketing at [Michigan State University](https://broad.msu.edu/)'s Broad College of Business. 
I bring a unique combination of academic credentials, having completed multiple degrees from top-ranked programs including an MS in Marketing Research from Michigan State University, an MS in Management Business Analytics and Intelligence from [Case Western Reserve University](https://case.edu/weatherhead/), and prior degrees in Psychology and Supply Chain Management with economic minor from Michigan State University. I work with [Prof. Hang Nguyen](https://broad.msu.edu/profile/htnguyen/) and [Prof. Forrest V. Morgeson III](https://broad.msu.edu/profile/morgeso3/).

## Research Interests
My research interests span marketing strategy, digital marketing, public policy, customer satisfaction, and causal inference.
